# SEO Content Architecture for the Five Authority Sites

Each website has exactly five non-overlapping catalogs:

1. Traffic catalog for informational search demand.
2. Education catalog for beginner frameworks and how-to content.
3. Solution catalog for actionable, product-aware content.
4. Review catalog for comparisons, alternatives, and buyer intent.
5. Lead-generation catalog for quizzes, checklists, templates, and email capture.

Every catalog can scale to 50-100 articles. The first ten evergreen article
ideas for every catalog are stored in `lib/content-architecture.ts` and rendered
on the matching catalog landing page.

## NeuroRestLab

**Brand mission:** Help readers build clearer days and calmer nights through
evidence-aware education and responsible product evaluation.

**Audience:** Busy adults researching focus, memory support, sleep quality, and
sustainable cognitive wellness routines.

**Pain points:** Brain fog and inconsistent focus; restless nights and low
daytime energy; confusing supplement and tool claims.

| Role | Catalog | Slug |

| Traffic | Brain Performance Insights | `brain-performance-insights` |

| Solution | Sleep Reset Protocols | `sleep-reset-protocols` |
| Review | Cognitive Product Reviews | `cognitive-product-reviews` |
| Lead generation | Brain & Sleep Assessment | `brain-sleep-assessment` |

**Keywords:** sleep quality, focus improvement, brain fog, memory support, deep
work routine, sleep reset, best sleep support tools, best focus supplements,

**Affiliate categories:** Sleep accessories, cognitive supplements, focus apps,
trackers, and educational programs.

educational sequence -> comparison guide -> responsible recommendation.

**Intent mix:** 60% informational, 30% commercial, 10% transactional.

## First 100 Articles

Use the same balanced launch roadmap for every website:

| Catalog role | Articles | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traffic | 30 | Build topical reach with informational long-tail keywords. |
| Education | 20 | Establish beginner frameworks and internal-link pathways. |
| Solution | 20 | Connect practical problems with product-aware next steps. |
| Review | 20 | Publish reviews, alternatives, and comparisons for buyer intent. |
| Lead generation | 10 | Launch quizzes, templates, checklists, and email assets. |

Publish in five 20-article sprints. Each sprint should include every catalog,
link informational articles into education and solution pages, and route
commercial readers into review pages and the quiz funnel.
